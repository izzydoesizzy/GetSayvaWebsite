41 Northcote Avenue

Sold
Aug 1
Written By Nalin Sharma

Stunning purpose-built luxury home in the heart of Queen West.

3000+ square feet featuring 4 spacious bedrooms and 5 bathrooms. Soaring 9 foot ceilings on the main, second and third floors. Large basement with 8 foot ceilings and a separate walk-out.

Open concept kitchen and family room-perfect for entertaining or relaxing with family. Sunny rooftop deck with clear views. 5.5 Kwh net metered solar power system and 10 Kwh Eco-Intelligent battery. This home has met rigorous Passive Home standards.

Close To West Queen West, Dundas West and Trinity Bellwoods Park.

Includes a Miele Appliance Package, Mitsubishi Zuba heating & cooling system, Rheem hybrid heat pump hot water tank. Heat Recovery Ventilation System for superior year-round air quality. Built-in speakers throughout. Nest thermostat and smoke detectors.

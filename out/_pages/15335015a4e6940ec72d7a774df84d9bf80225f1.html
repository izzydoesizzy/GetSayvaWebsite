2212 Lake Shore Unit 1808

Sold
Aug 4
Written By Nalin Sharma

Calling all outdoor enthusiasts! Lush parks, walking trails and bike paths await at this sunny, corner unit at Humber Bay Shores.

Featuring 9.5 foot ceilings, near floor-to ceiling windows, 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and a separate den that'll make the perfect home office. The split bedroom floorplan is highly functional with no wasted space. Gorgeous views of summer greenery and the lake.

State-of-the-art W Club gym and yoga studio with free classes. Other amenities include a pool, steam room, sauna, hot tub, squash courts and rooftop terrace with BBQ’s.

Metro, LCBO, Shoppers, banks and other shops on ground level. Walk to the Lakeshore in under 10 minutes. And don’t forget the Humber Bay Shores farmers market every Saturday!

5 minute drive or shuttle bus to Mimico GO Station. Quick access to Gardiner, 5 minutes to highway 427, and 18 minutes to Pearson Airport.

Bathrooms: 2
